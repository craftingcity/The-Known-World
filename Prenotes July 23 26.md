### Intro

When last we left our heroes, their explorations of this level of Kailkeep led them into a guarded hall, seemingly unmonitored by the Kobolds above. In contentious contact with these guards, you learned that they hail from the City of Mercy - a supposed settlement of civil folk in this savage hell. In this talk, however, the tension boiled over for the Great Dragoon, who launched a fireball at these unknown contenders, provoking Steve to try to seize him. 

The Guards rushed to re-enter the city, taking Mip and Steve alongside them, and the Great Dragoon was forced to invoke his great arcane talents to allow himself and the rest of your party a quiet entry into the city. 

The doors are slammed shut behind you, and barred closed quickly after. You stand now observing a marketplace, with buildings, and people - none of whom are immediately fighting you or each other. For the first time in a long time, you are in a City. A civil collective. A home of people. For the first time in a long time, you feel slightly safe.

The guard who spoke with you before speaks to you; "Don't fight, and don't steal. If you can do that, you can stay. Moihra's Bar is right across the street there, you should talk with her about a room. We'll catch up with you later. Might have some more questions for you."
