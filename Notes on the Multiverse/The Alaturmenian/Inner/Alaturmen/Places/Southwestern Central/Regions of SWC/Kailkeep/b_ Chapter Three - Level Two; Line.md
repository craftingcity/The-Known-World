# Level Two: Line
The second level of Kailkeep extends the ruse of the first; continuing in the "dwarven style", the second level provides additional artistic pieces that more explicitly tell the story of the imaginary dwarves, and a .

Explicitly, the second level tells the tale (in pictograms and [[Montal (language)]]) of discovering and claiming this place for [[Mountain Hall]], including self-indulgent references to a "powerful kobold clan" that continually presents issues to the dwarves of the story. 

## What Dwells Here?
### Loyalists
Loyalists in the second level are more willing to engage in combat than they are on the first level. This is largely due to the presence of the [[#Armory]], where the strongest among them will attempt to congregate and prepare for battle to defend the weakest.

While not retreating from, preparing for, or participating in a combat, the Loyalists here will pray to Terra, work in the shops below, and gather for small party-shows in the [[#Dining & Entertaining Room]].

### Unusual Rodents
A population of Unusual Rodents has escaped containment and survived the meager hunts of the loyalists.

## Exploring this Place
As the complex continues, a series of chambers connect above and below to continually produce a varied combination of the following types of rooms.

### Navigation
*Level Two* is accessed by ascending one of the many staircases below or scaling up to the [[#Balconies]] from the attached *Grand Chamber*. 

*Level Two* can be exited by ascending the stairs present in the [[#Simple Barracks]] or [[#Balconies]].

### Detail
#### Simple Barracks
The Simple Barracks is a long rectangular room filled with stone and hay bunk-beds where the population of the Allures rest. Small footlockers lie at each of the bunks, containing small personal items and a change of clothes.

Each end of the room opens to another by a set of stone double-doors.

#### Balconies
The Balconies are an identical pair of simple viewing areas that overlook the *Grand Chamber* below. They are lined in ornate stone railings, and play host to a pair of stone benches. Of the two single-wide doors in the walls of the balconies, one goes to a descending staircase, while the other goes to an ascending staircase.
