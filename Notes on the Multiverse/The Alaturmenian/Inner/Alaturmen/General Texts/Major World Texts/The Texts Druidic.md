The Texts Druidic are a collection of resources created and maintained by the [[Circles of Idols]] that describe the philosophies, abilities, and histories of each of the five circles of the individual idols. This massive tome of lore acts as the basis of Modern Arcane Theory, as the many axioms and theories of the Weave Arcane are first published in this work, although they are presented poorly in the Modern understanding.

The [[Druids of Animan]] is responsible for the publicization of this work, much to the chagrin of the other Circles.
