# Dwarven Kingdom Actstow
## History
```
### Dwarven Kingdom Actstow

The Dwarven Kingdom of Actstow is a monarchy supported by family clans of miners and farmers. These clans are as ancient as Alaturmen, and claim that their ancestors were carved from the stone here, a hundred or more years ago.

  

Actstow is very condensed, there is very little available space, public or private. People from here bask in the luxury of privacy and cling close to their comrades. Many cultures interact with the Dwarven Kingdom Actstow on great terms, including [[#Cenva Minyo]] to their south, [[#Kingdom of Prosper]] to their east, and [[Deepburrow]] to their *below*.

  

The culture of Actstow is one of Neutral Good. The people here are no stranger to evading the law, tax evasion is a specialty, and bodily harm or physical restrictions are never the correct answer when you or yours have been wronged.

## Actstow / Cenva Minyo
You spent your life before adventure in the [[Actstow Hills]]. You may have lived in a home within the suspended forest village, or in the tunnels of the hills themselves. The [[Dwarven Kingdom Actstow]] - a growing industrial powerhouse - would take in a large population of refugees as the [[Great Fires]] began and continued. These refugees, largely of Winter Elven heritage, would eventually go on to form [[Cenva Minyo]], a state whose sole purpose is the abolition of [[Its Greatness|The Great White Flame]] and it's [[The Church of the White Flame|White Church]]. The [[Dwarven Kingdom Actstow]] and [[Cenva Minyo]] have grown into a mutually beneficial co-habitual relationship that has led the rest of the world to regard them both as one paired half of the other. Together, they support [[The Kingdom of Prosper]] in their efforts against the [[Blood Clans]] and the [[The Church of the White Flame|White Church]].
```

The Kingdom of Actstow was carved from the stone of the Hills of the same name in the first years of history on Alaturmen. They developed quickly, though unlike their [[Mountain Hall|sister state in the Mountains]], they held little colonial ambition - preferring to remain in the comfort of non-conflict.

When the [[Great Fires]] came to the North West, and [[The Empire that Never Was]] faltered and failed, it's refugees would largely find themselves moving into the [[Actstow Hills]], and attempting recovery in the forests above. As these new people came, they were welcomed with open arms, assisted and homed, fed and formalized into a state unto themselves. We now call them [[Cenva Minyo]], and together, these two states have remained boiling with potency.

## Governance
The Kingdom of Actstow is an aristocratic monarchy of noble clans, led by a single King.
