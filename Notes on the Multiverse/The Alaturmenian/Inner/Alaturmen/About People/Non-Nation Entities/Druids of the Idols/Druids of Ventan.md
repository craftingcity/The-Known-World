# Druids of Ventan
## History
The influence of the Druids of Ventan is most visible within [[Northeastern Central]] Alaturmen, the area we now know as [[Arthur's Prairie]] and the [[Illburn Forest]], as the group has occupied the region for all of recorded history.

As the [[Bandit Lords]] came to power in the region, the Druids of Ventan were heavily persecuted; the most visible among them were hunted and slaughtered, and those who could hide did.
