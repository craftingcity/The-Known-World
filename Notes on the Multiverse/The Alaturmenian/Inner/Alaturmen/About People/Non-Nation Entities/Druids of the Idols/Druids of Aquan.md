# Druids of Aquan
## History
The Druids of Aquan are most visible within [[Southeastern Central]] Alaturmen, the area we now know as [[The Democratic Combine of Peoples]], having occupied the region to some degree for all of recorded history.

As the [[Bandit Lords]] came to expand into the region around the turn of the second decade, the Druids of Aquan were forced into a tenuous non-aggression pact with their new Lords where, in exchange for their continued existence, the Aquans would first bless the lands of their new Lords with great fertility, and then remove themselves from the cities of their Lords, never to return. 

Although they were quite proactive in the destruction of the treacherous gluttons that had subjugated them, they have upheld their agreement with the Bandit Lords to this day, still refusing to set foot within any walled settlement in the Southeast.
