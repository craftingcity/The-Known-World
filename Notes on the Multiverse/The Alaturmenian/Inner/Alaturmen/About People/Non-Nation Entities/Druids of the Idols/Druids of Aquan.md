# Druids of Aquan
## History
The Druids of Aquan are most visible within [[Southeastern Central]] Alaturmen, the area we now know as [[The Democratic Combine of Peoples]], having occupied the region to some degree for all of recorded history.

As the [[Bandit Lords]] came to expand into the region around the turn of the second decade, the Druids of Aquan were forced into a tenuous non-aggression pact with their new Lords where, in exchange for their continued existence, the Aquans would first bless the lands of their new Lords with great fertility with the blood of their eldest, and then remove themselves from the cities of their Lords, never to return. 

Although they were quite proactive in the destruction of the treacherous gluttons that had subjugated them, they have upheld their agreement with the [[Bandit Lords]] to this day, still refusing to set foot within any walled settlement in the Southeast. Given this decision, and their thinly-spread presence throughout the region, the Druids of Aquan act independently of any Baron of the region, and together form a formidable information gathering network that is highly valued by those who can access it.

## Governance
The Druids of Aquan typically gather in groups of 5-20 to form a Den, and are led by the eldest among them. Each Den acts independently, but their connection to the Idol of Water pushes them all in the same direction, even when they take different paths.

Many Dens will individually come to an agreement with the Barons that claim the area they live within, gaining access to the bounties of the cities in exchange for information - political, magical, or otherwise. These agreements are typically immediate and non-continuous, as the Aquans prefer not to deal over time.
