# Druids of Terran
## History
The influence of the Druids of Terran is less visible than the other Circles, for a number of storied reasons, though it can be felt in [[Northwestern Central]] Alaturmen, the area we now know as [[The Holyl'nds]], as the group has occupied the region to the best of their ability for all of recorded history.

The Terrans would come to work hand-in-hand with the first Elves of the region to build [[The Empire that Never Was]].
