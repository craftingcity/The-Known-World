# Druids of Ignan
## History
The influence of the Druids of Ignan is most visible within [[Southwestern Central]] Alaturmen, the area we now know as [[The Deserts of Tetrimoc]], as the group has occupied the region - more particularly [[The Golden City]] - for all of recorded history.

As the Planebearer, the Great Lightbringer, [[Tetrimoc]] graced the Deserts of our world with his presence, the Druids of Ignan came to flock around him. Being the first of his Congregation, the Ignans would come to occupy positions of great influence in the region, becoming captains, priests, and scholars under the reign of the Golden Dragon.

As the region fractured in need of food and water, and the Noble Thieves began to come into their own, the Ignans would maintain their grasp upon the region by positioning themselves just under the leaders of influence in the world of the Thieves. Finding none more pious than another, the Ignans have quietly locked the three gangs into a tight stalemate of skirmishing and survival.
