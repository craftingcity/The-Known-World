# Druids of Ignan
## History
The influence of the Druids of Ignan is most visible within [[Southwestern Central]] Alaturmen, the area we now know as [[The Deserts of Tetrimoc]], as the group has occupied the region - more particularly [[The Golden City]] - for all of recorded history.

As the Planebearer, the Great Lightbringer, [[Tetrimoc]] graced the Deserts of our world with his presence, the Druids of Ignan came to flock around him. Being the first of his Congregation, the Ignans occupied positions of great influence in the region, becoming the first captains, priests, merchants, and scholars under the reign of the Golden Dragon.

As the region fractured in need of food and water, and the Noble Thieves began to come into their own, the Ignans would maintain their grasp upon the region by positioning themselves just under the leaders of influence in the world of the Thieves. Finding none more pious than another, the Ignans have quietly locked the three gangs into a tight stalemate of skirmishing and survival, awaiting the change they know will come.

## Governance
The Druids of Ignan typically do not gather, and rather act together by independently consulting the Flames of a campfire, stove, candle, or other inconspicuous source of fire for the information necessary to act upon the will of their Circle and their Idol.

The Druids of Ignan await *something*, an obvious and irreversible change coming to the face of Alaturmen, and while they have together prepared for whatever this change may be, they hold dearly the acceptance that they may not live to see their plan come to fruition - everything is impermanent, after all.
