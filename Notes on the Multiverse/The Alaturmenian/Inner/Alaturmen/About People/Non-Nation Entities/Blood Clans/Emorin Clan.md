# Emorin Clan
## History
The Emorin Clan claims to be the originator of the [[Blood-Hobgoblin Clan Pact]], citing their own constitution and the placement of their family names upon the Clan Pact. The Emorin Clan has been a bureaucracy in the [[Gumlpin]] region for all of history, and their Merchant Bands serve to supply each region with the goods of another, while pocketing a small cut of the exchange - of course.

Having lived in the region for so long, one might expect their holdings to be tall or wide, wonderous in any case, but they are rather dull and utilitarian in comparison to most other regions of the world. Few large public works are present in their territories, and those that do are in reverence to the many deities of the world more than they are for the projection of power or prestige. The wealthiest people live in their own apartment, work in their own station, and sell their own products - and those who can't work for themselves work for someone else.
