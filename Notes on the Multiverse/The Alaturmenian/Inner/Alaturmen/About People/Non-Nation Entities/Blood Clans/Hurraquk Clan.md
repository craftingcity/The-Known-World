# Hurraquk Clan
## History
Having split the Wakquk Clan in twain following the death of her mother, [[Lily-Mae Garqwak]] named herself Mengugu of the Clan in contention with her sister, and lines were drawn. Gathering the will of the people of the west, Marley would eventually be forced to flee the capital city of what was once the Wakquk Claim, the [[Forest-City of Garqwuk]], and found her legacy in the [[Hurraquk Hillkeep]]. The clan has reached a tenuous peace with it's sister clan, the [[Garqwak Clan]], since which the [[Wakquk Twins]] have refused to speak, alliances be damned.
