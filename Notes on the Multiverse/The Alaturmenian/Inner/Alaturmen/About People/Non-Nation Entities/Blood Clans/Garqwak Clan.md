# Garqwak Clan
## History
Having split the Waquk Clan in twain following the death of her mother, [[Marley Hurraquk]] named herself Mengugu of the Clan in contention with her sister, and lines were drawn. Gathering those who claim to be 'loyalist' to the will of [[Gail Wakquk]], Lily-Mae would hold the "capital city" of what was once the Wakquk Clan's territories, the [[Forest-City of Garqwuk]], against the western infidels and oathbreakers supposedly responsible for Gail's death. The clan has reached a tenuous peace with it's sister clan, the [[Garqwak Clan]], since which the [[Wakquk Twins]] have refused to speak, alliances be damned.
