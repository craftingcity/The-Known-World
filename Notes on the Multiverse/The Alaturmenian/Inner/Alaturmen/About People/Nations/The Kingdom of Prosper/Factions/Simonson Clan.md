# Simonson Clan
## History
The Simonson clan was once a feared clan of militants building a nation in what we have come to name [[Arthur's Prairie]]. [[Amelia Simonson]] - the head of house in her time - birthed a son, [[Arthur Simonson|Artie]], and for a long time, he cared little for the politics of his home. As he returned from his days of adventuring, he found his home in ruin, his people deeply scarred. He knew it was only the fault of no-one, only the lack of a truly righteous leader following the death of his Mother. Members of the Simonsons' signatures are present on the [[Blood-Hobgoblin Clan Pact]], and although their clan was pushed out of power by their peers, through violence, [[Arthur Simonson]] argued that he was a legal participant in the [[Blood-Hobgoblin Clan Pact]] and should be afforded it's rights. After much litigation, and loss of life, love, and lore, the other clans agreed.

Since the [[Unification]], the "Simonson Clan" has become a legal entity more than a group of families, being the culturally appropriate name of [[The Kingdom of Prosper]] within the [[Blood Clans]] territories.
