# Avernus, First Layer of Hell
#### A sub-region of [[Baator]]
## Boundaries
## Contents
> The first layer is Baator's largest beachhead, it has been ravaged by the [[Blood Wars of Fiends]]. Legions of [[Devils]] marched to repel the hordes of [[Demons]] from their home, and have been at an ever-roiling-stalemate since.

Avernus is a cavernous plane of red rock and black metals. Jagged, and sharp, much of the surface of Avernus reeks of battle; the touch of sulphur, iron, and death assault your eyes, nose, and mouths, and the ground is blasted up and around, forming deep craters and tall hills. Bodies of ruined war-machines litter the layer, half destroyed, half scrapped for parts. 

Avernus is a place of war, ruled by the Arch-Devil [[Istratige]] and their cabinet of generals. Here, they fight against the never-ending tide of Demons crossing into Avernus from the [[#Beachhead Styx]] or the [[#Spikelands]].
