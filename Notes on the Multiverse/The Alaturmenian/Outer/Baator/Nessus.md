# Nessus, Ninth Layer of Hell
#### A sub-region of [[Baator]]
> The layer is a featureless plain with jagged edges that extend 2,500 miles from east to west and 1,100 miles from north to south, floating in an endless misty-red void. These plains are scarred by chasms and ravines several thousand miles deep, and are frequently ravaged by fiery storm winds.
