# Mechanis
## The Outer Plane of Ideal Law
Mechanis is the plane of Law and - at least in part - the engine of our reality and the reason for it's cohesion. As souls come to rest in Mechanis they are reformed into Mecha, the independent arms of the plane's will, and eventually assigned their duties in maintaining reality.

Being the plane of Ideal Law, Mechanis is perfectly formed to it's innumerable tasks, where each task has a home and a team to work it.

### Regulatory
Central to the plane in perception and function, the Regulatory is the home-base of the Regulators, who are charged with the correction of mistakes, misappropriations, and malpractices of reality and continuity throughout the multiverse. 

While certainly some petty disputes are filed into the everlasting backlog of cases to be pursued, the Regulators are tasked first and foremost with the correction of the most major issues, such as the disruption or perversion of space-time, the dissuasion of entropy or natural failures, or the pursuit of unmaking reality.

### New Cannane
Surrounding the Regulatory is New Cannane, a city-like structure of purposeful sections that shift within and around one another to connect the sections and their inhabitants while they would require it. The sections of New Cannane are where most Mecha will spend their existences, performing their duties with everlasting purpose.
