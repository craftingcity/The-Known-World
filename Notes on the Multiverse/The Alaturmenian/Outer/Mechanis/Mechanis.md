# Mechanis
## The Outer Plane of Ideal Law
Mechanis is the plane of Law and - at least in part - the engine of our reality and the reason for it's cohesion. As souls come to rest in Mechanis they are reformed into Mecha, the independent arms of the plane's will, and eventually assigned their duties in maintaining reality.

Being the plane of Ideal Law, Mechanis is perfectly formed to it's innumerable tasks, where each task has a home and a team to work it.

### Regulatory
Central to the plane in perception and function, the Regulatory is the home of the Regulators, who are charged with the correction of mistakes, misappropriations, and malpractices throughout the multiverse. 

While certainly some petty disputes are filed into the everlasting backlog of cases to be pursued, the Regulators are tasked first and foremost with the correction of more major issues, such as the disruption or perversion of space-time, the dissuasion of entropy or natural failures, or the pursuit of unmaking reality.
