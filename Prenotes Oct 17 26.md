### Intro

When last we left our heroes, the party fractured upon the rocks of Freedom. In dealing with the aftermath of their battle with a gang of Kobolds, ten **Tokens** were to be inserted into the machine, with hopes that the powers-that-be would release you all from this place for this sacrifice. Chaos broke as the tenth was inserted, Dragoon and Steve physically clashing over what really the group was purchasing. In this chaos, Mip walked forward to the machine, now full and ready, and took her chance to escape.

As she pulled the lever before her, the ground dropped from under her. Freedom for one, not all. 

Unless you can strike a deal. Dragoon has opened a connection to the Idol here before, so why not try again?

He offers his devotion for their Freedom. This is not a selfless act - and the rest of us can see it in his eyes. He needs Steve to stick around, so he needs the Civilians to leave. He needs power, so he must serve those who possess it. Two for the price of one, and the ground opens again.

Many of the civilians you've brought along with you have died or swiftly clambered down and away, into their 'Freedom'. The rest have accompanied the Roamer, Tynan, back to Mercy. You are left with the ever-loyal Goblin #1, the other two Roamers, Screw and Sierra, and the Operator who so foolishly attempted to stop your ascension.

You have taken this desperately horrified Kobold to the door that will either blow off his hand or finally open, and found that this one is indeed the key for this particular lock.

As it turns, and the grinding of stone against stone reveals tight tunnels before you, the moment of awe washing over the party is interrupted by the thudding of boots and a call behind you;

> EMILIO; 'Ey, wait for me!'

### What does the World Know?

We're headed into Kailkeep Proper, specifically the tunnels around *Hauro*. Terra, and her "cell", are located even above *Yuub*, but she'll be able to work her magics more obviously as we continue to ascend.

Kailkeep Proper responds as a Hivemind to a degree, especially in that it is willing to remove a significant portion of itself as long as some part continues to survive. This means their direct action will likely involve attempting to corner any intruders and then either they release themselves via one of many exits onto the surface-side, or they are contained and quickly killed (via gassing, heat/cold, or if all else fails dehydration).
