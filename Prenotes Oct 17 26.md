### Intro

When last we left our heroes, the party fractured upon the rocks of Freedom. In dealing with the aftermath of their battle with a gang of Kobolds, ten **Tokens** were to be inserted into the machine, with hopes that the powers-that-be would release you all from this place for this sacrifice. Chaos broke as the tenth was inserted, Dragoon and Steve physically clashing over what really the group was purchasing. In this chaos, Mip walked forward to the machine, now full and ready, and took her chance to escape.

As she pulled the lever before her, the ground dropped from under her. Freedom for one, not all. 

Unless you can strike a deal. Dragoon has opened a connection to the Idol here before, so why not try again?

He offers his devotion for their Freedom. This is not a selfless act - and the rest of us can see it in his eyes. He needs Steve to stick around, so he needs the Civilians to leave. He wants power, so he offers to serve the highest station he can find.
