### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they had returned to their home in [[Coalition City]] for a period of well deserved rest. Each of you have your own goals to pursue during this time, but a united front presents itself in a journey to [[Mountain Hall]] in search of a conversation with a renown fiendologist and one of the few surviving members of [[The Hatdroppers]]; the dwarf named [[Fulton Shepherd]]. Before this journey begins, however, we must discuss the happenings of the house. Being tasked with the largest share of the chores of running your adventuring party, Anita has delegated the information gathering tasks to Roan - who has in turn worked their reputation with the Slimknives and your heavy pockets into a team of investigators on the hunt - and begun the cultivation of your library's fiendology section, including Infernal Language learning guides and a hopefully comprehensive profile of fiendish entities.

All of this will take time. Time that you so efficiently have decided to utilize in deeper preparation; if there is anything you want to buy, or questions you'd like to ask, before you travel westward into the [[Deakred Range]], now is the time.

### What does the World Know?
#### Going to see Shepard
[[Fulton Shepherd]] is living in [[Mountain Hall]] at the Temple of Flowers. He is a Paladin of Gish, and has experience in fighting against demons, which he extends to apply to devils. He doesn't know these folks, so he'll ask the party to do a gang clear in the area (vs the 'Loyalists') before he'll talk. His advice would include information about damages / weakness of fiends and on Planeswalking - advising that it is dangerous, but the most accessible method of reaching the outer planes, where the Arbiter is likely based.
