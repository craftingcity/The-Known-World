### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they were exploring the rank depths of the Lone Mountain, [[Kailkeep]]; having followed the route presented to you by your comrade captor, you have ascended onto the Fourth Level of this hellish gameshow. You have won your game of 'Red Rover', and now have seen the brutal benefits of victory here, where bounties follow your spilling of blood. You have passed a small Oasis here, and an odd machine, having explored a small section of this layer. 

You have sent your minion, who we call "Goblin #1", to scout forward and spread word of your power and ferocity. You have met a few of the people who inhabit this place, in battle and in conversation, for as little as they spoke. 

Now, you stand now together, but alone, in your make-shift camp. You are rested. The Machine has quieted some time ago, leaving the small waterflow as the only constant noise now. You've seen a half dozen doors you've left unexplored. 

The only way out, is through. What do you do?
