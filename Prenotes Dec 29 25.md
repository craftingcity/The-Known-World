### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they had escaped their conflict in [[The Holyl'nds]] and turned to [[Coalition City]] for a period of recovery, where they engaged in commerce and conversation with the citizens of the City. Their eyes were caught, for reasons each their own, by a series of board-postings that alluded to some ongoings in [[The Deserts of Tetrimoc]], at the legendary mountain we call [[Kailkeep]].

In addition to the legend you know - that the draconic god-king [[Tetrimoc]] was cut upon the tip of the mountain upon his descent into Alaturmen - the postage suggested that a large bounty of gold and magic awaits those who would come to claim it, but also that a number of people are concerned that their friends or family have gone missing around that area.

Armed with this information and the equipment you procured in [[Coalition City]], you have travelled west-north-west along the [[Deakred Range]] and into the Desert. Today is the 6th day of Drying in the 106th year, and before you stands the large and lonely stone mass we call [[Kailkeep]].
