### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they had found themselves in the presence of the only community they've seen in quite some time - the city of Mercy, clinging onto civility by a thread in this barbaric hell we call Kailkeep. Here they spoke to the citizens of a fulfilling life, of their dreams beyond the walls of the mountain, of the people who might still be awaiting their return, to which one of their leaders - the Captain of the Roamers, Sierra - would speak of the horrors that trying will cause. She spoke of safety, of risk, of the ever-present threat of exposure and execution, and, in other words, the threat of the unknown. 

This speech carried out by your party led a number of civilians to volunteer, to join you on your journey towards the intangible idea of an escape, which in turn, led Sierra herself to tag along with the express intent of returning the people of Mercy to their homes once you fail them. She does not believe in you, and she has made her stance clear; Sierra will help you, but the safety of her dependents is her first priority.

You have collected what little materials could help you, and you all - including the dozen or so civilians you have convinced - stand now at the edge of town, by the kill-box gate you entered through.

Your gathered speak of tokens and kobolted doors, of traps and tricks and monsters to best. The people are excited to leave, and terrified to try, but you have reignited the spark of hope they once lost.

The plan you have for now is theory. The idea of a route. And you know that you will be watched, and prodded, and toyed with by the sadistic keepers of this "game" of blood and pain. But the alternative is leaving everyone here to rot, and shrivel into themselves in a mountain that only wishes them suffering for eternity.

What do you do?
