### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they had arrived in a City - the first of it's kind since they've come to this place. Mercy lies stagnant and depressed, but clinging to life. After settling in, and seeing the state of the people here, it was decided that a speech would be held by the Great Dragoon in the Town Hall in an attempt to rally those brave enough - or foolish enough - to join you in attempting an escape from this evil place - for good.

In speaking to two of the three Councilmembers of this town, you found Sierra, Head of Roamers, very resistant to your idea. So resistant in fact, that she has decided to not only speak in opposition to the Great Dragoon, but follow you in your attempt to escape - only so that she might watch you fail. 

Word is being spread of your speech, and it's purpose, as you have commanded it to be, and the town has begun to buzz. Where once you received whispers and defensive looks, you now receive a smile, or a nod, or even sometimes a wave. You have very quickly developed a positive reputation by showing your interest and splashing your coin around town, and people seem to be very excited about the upcoming speech.

You are wherever you need to be to prepare. After dinner, you will speak, and Mercy will listen. What comes next is yet to be seen. 

What do you do?
