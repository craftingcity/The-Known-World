### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they had overcome a mighty challenger in Bernet the Lodestone, a golem of iron in a room of magnets, and gathered together his large collection of Iridescent **Tokens**. Although their truest purpose remains mysterious, upon entering the Scoreboard room attached to Bernet's Lair, some of their secrets were revealed.

You found that, on this level, the Scoreboard room has a depository for these **Tokens**, displaying a number above itself that you assume to be the amount currently present within the device, a few sentences embossed into the front provide more context to those who can decipher it; the text, written in [[Draconic (language)]], reads "Safety costs One. Progress costs Three. Freedom costs Ten."

The Great Dragoon, being the only one among you who understands the language before you, has decided to keep the translation of the final sentence of these lines to himself, and too his reason for doing so.

Nevertheless, you chose to leave this depository for now, turning to focus on the well-being of yourselves and your party. In addition to the procurement of many gifts, you took the time to perform an... enhancement surgery. After Goblin #1 lost his second arm, his martial prowess suffered greatly, and thus his usefulness as a member of your party (to the miniscule degree that he is) suffered as well. To remedy this ailment you all now suffer, you have opened the end of his heavily maimed dominant arm, rammed the handle of your newest, fanciest sword into this gaping wound, and then tried your hardest to secure it's place and return Goblin #1 to good health and fighting potential. During the course of his surgery, he fell into a seizure that has left him alive, but currently unresponsive, effectively rendering your ally comatose until further notice - but he has a sword for an arm now.

We return to this moment. You stand in a Scoreboard room, knowing that this place sprawls out around you, and that danger might lurk behind every door. Your ally lies on the floor before you, alive, but useless still, and you clutch your **Token** earnings close.

What do you do?
