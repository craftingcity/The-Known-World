### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they had continued their struggle in glory and horror against the will of the Kobolds of Kailkeep and their sadistic game. Your most recent bout of victory was brought by your slaying of some dozen or more people in a game that was called 'Four Corners', though it resembled the game of your youth in setting alone. Following your bloody victory, you were directed to ascend from the room in the central cage that had descended to begin that game, and you did. "And with that, *please* welcome... Dragoons Greats! You know 'em, you love 'em, and they have just two~ minutes to... Escape! The! Room!"
