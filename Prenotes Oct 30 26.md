### Intro

Today, we gather around this table to tell a story. A story of people. A story of heroes. Of their actions, their feelings, and their destinies. Together, we will tell a story of three people, who's meeting was unlikely, and who's lives will irrevocably change in the presence of each other.

The state of the world is tense. Not quite perilous, but the events of the last few years have left everyone with a well-founded sense of unease. Once, some dozen years ago, the largest Nations of this world were united under the banner of the [[Guilds of Coalition]] - a united front against the worlds beyond our own. They have since failed us. 

War has come to the North, pitching [[The Church of the White Flame]] against [[The Kingdom of Prosper]], halting the oh-so vital routes of trade that cross these two nations. Some unknowable infection ravages the [[Deakred Range]] and the people of the [[Dwarven Kingdom Suremantle]], as rumors speak of tentacular horrors and mithril-madness spreading through the mountains. The southern states too have been thrust into hardship; Barons of the [[The Democratic Combine of Peoples|DCP]] have been murdered - some by extraplanar entities, some by corporate mercenaries - and the rallying cry of the South-East Alliance of Security continues to be forcibly spread throughout the region. The deserts stand as harsh as they have ever been. Having been the battleground of the first demonic invasion our world has known, little pressure has been applied to the status-que of economic castigation
