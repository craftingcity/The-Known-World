### Intro

Today, we gather around this table to tell a story. A story of people. A story of heroes. Of their actions, their feelings, and their destinies. Together, we will tell a story of three people, who's meeting was unlikely, and who's lives will irrevocably change in the presence of each other.

The state of the world is tense. Not quite perilous, but the events of the last few years have left everyone with a well-founded sense of unease. Once, some dozen years ago, the largest Nations of this world were united under the banner of the [[Guilds of Coalition]] - a united front against the worlds beyond our own. They have since failed us. 

War has come to the North, pitching [[The Church of the White Flame]] against [[The Kingdom of Prosper]], halting the oh-so vital routes of trade that cross these two nations. Some unknowable infection ravages the [[Deakred Range]] and the people of the [[Dwarven Kingdom Suremantle]], as rumors speak of tentacular horrors and mithril-madness spreading through the mountains. The southern states too have been thrust into hardship; Barons of the [[The Democratic Combine of Peoples|DCP]] have been murdered - some by extraplanar entities, some by corporate mercenaries - and the rallying cry of the South-East Alliance of Security continues to be forcibly spread throughout the region. 

The deserts stand as harsh as they have ever been. Since having been the battleground of the first demonic invasion our world has known, little pressure has been applied to the status-que of economic castigation held and enforced by the [[Golden Claws]] of [[The Deserts of Tetrimoc]]. Some political rumblings, questions of who the [[Houran Venclaw|High Ambassador]] is truly speaking for, but little of note has changed. Until today.

Today is the 20th of Harvest, in the 106th year. And today is the first day in a half-century that a living Dragon has flown upon the face of Alaturmen. Coming from the East, somewhere in the DCP, a Golden-winged reptile flew across the [[Alvalini River]], above the Shrublands and the Blessings, before it came to roost on the western edge of the world. Today, a genuine Dragon has landed somewhere in the [[Lord's Folly]].
