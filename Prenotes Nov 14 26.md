### Intro

When last we left our heroes, the party had travelled into [[Baator]], the plane of Hell, and traversed a war-torn wasteland to reach Outpost Afierono. There, they met a devil calling itself Timir who offered warnings for their souls and guidance to their destination. Quickly, negotiations broke down between the party and their devil, who - in our eyes - failed to accept a reasonable payment for the services offered, or refused to offer the services we truly require. Conflict ensues, and our party is forced to flee Afierono further into Hell - losing their trusted ally Roan along the way.

You've run through the gates, and farther beyond down a long path. The fiends have stopped chasing you, but the stone of this path shifts under your feet, making slowing difficult, and stopping impossible. The mountainous walls grow up and around you, forming a tunnel and forcing you through it until the orange glow of the end grows larger and brighter and eventually engulfs you.

Even before you can blink away the blinding light, your senses tell you that you have become elsewhere. The sound of distant rumbling has been replaced with a cacophonous bustle of people moving and shouting. Burning smog replaces the once ever-present smell of sulphur, and as your eyes clear, you confirm your suspicions. You find yourself in a city. Before you, buildings of brass and stone stand tall, and taller still towards the center of the city. Your surroundings imply you are currently in an industrial district, as smithies bang metal into shape, and carts are drug to-and-fro along the street.

In your immediate area, the most recognizable building is the tavern on the corner - the glowing sign is illegible to you, but the iconography of a freshly poured ale is hard to miss. Further down the street, some two-hundred feet away, laborers drag their carts around a brawl without intervening.

As lost as you were before, you are now swamped in options. What do you do?
