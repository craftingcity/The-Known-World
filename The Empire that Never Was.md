"The Empire that Never Was", also known as the First Empire or the Ancient Empire, was the shining hope of the [[Ceilie Courts]] on the face of [[Alaturmen]].

As the Courts sent the Elves to take [[Alaturmen]], they would send them with plans for a widespread Empire, made in the image of the Courts, and prosperous for all things of the land. Some progress was made in the twenty-odd years that the Empire was afforded, such as the wonderous city of [[The Old|Minyostalma]], but their idealist expansion was quickly halted by the [[Great Fires]] of 023AF.

Now, only the ruins of progress remain, occupied by bandits, heretics, and mercenaries alike. [[The Old|Minyostalma]] stands, although now poisoned and decrepit, as the image of what could have been for the world, if only that fiery intervention did not come.
