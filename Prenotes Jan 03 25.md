### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they had traveled to the Temple of Flowers in [[Mountain Hall]] to meet with the renown fiendologist [[Fulton Shepherd]]. Once arrived, he asked that you remove a group of 'Loyalists' from a local hangout - which you promptly set ablaze. In return, he discussed with you your plans in regard to finding and destroying the Arbiter known to you as 'the Notadeer'. 

For as little as you know about the Hells of [[Baator]] and it's Devils, you have been told that they follow a strict bureaucracy, one that would keep an incident report or a receipt of some kind - hopefully containing some actionable information regarding the Arbiter you seek. Fulton has suggested that, once you are as prepared as you can be, you might planeswalk from Alaturmen and into Baator, through the layers it contains, to either the record or the Arbiter, and then return, victorious.

A suggestion had been floated too, that before you would travel into [[Baator]], you would summon a Lesser Devil
