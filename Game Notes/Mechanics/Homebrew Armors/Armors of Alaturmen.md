#### Chain Shirt

Created with 15 pounds of Metal.

#### Scale Mail
A suit of layered metal armor that covers much of the body in small scales, allowing more flexibility at the cost of effective protection, worn over a comfortable undersuit.

Created with 6 sq. yards of Textiles and 45 pounds of Metal.

#### Breastplate
A shirt of hearty platemail that protects the vital organs, but little else. Worn over a suit of simple Leather armor (sold separately).

Created with 25 pounds of Metal.

#### Halfplate
A half-suit of hearty platemail that protects the vital organs, and much of the front of a fighter, worn over a thick leather undersuit.

Created with 6 sq. yards of Textiles and 40 pounds of Metal.

#### Ringmail
A suit of leather armor reinforced by metal rings and bars placed across the most vulnerable points of contact.

Created with 8 sq. yards of Textiles and 25 pounds of Metals.

#### Chainmail
A suit of full chainmail. Protects the whole body.

Created with 35 pounds of Metals.

#### Splintmail
A suit of leather armor onto which strips of metal have been riveted. The joints are additionally protected by lengths of chainmail.

Created with 8 sq. yards of Textiles and 50 pounds of Metals.

#### Platemail
A suit of hearty platemail that protects the whole body in thick metal sheets.

Created with 4 sq. yards of Textiles and 75 pounds of Metals.
