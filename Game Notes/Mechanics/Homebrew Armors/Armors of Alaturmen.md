#### Leather
A suit of padded armor that covers the full body in thick and protective, yet flexing and comfortable leather.

Created with 8 sq. yards of Textiles. 

#### Studded
A suit of padded armor that covers the full body in thick and protective, yet flexing and comfortable leather that has been fitted with reinforcing metal studs.

Created with 8 sq. yards of Textiles and 10 pounds of Metal.

#### Hide
A suit of padded armor that covers the full body in heavy natural furs and hides.

Created with 10 sq. yards of Textiles.

#### Chain Shirt
A shirt of chainmail that protects the vital organs, but little else. Worn over a suit of simple Leather armor (sold separately).

Created with 15 pounds of Metal.

#### Scale Mail
A suit of layered metal armor that covers much of the body in small scales, allowing more flexibility at the cost of effective protection, worn over a comfortable undersuit.

Created with 6 sq. yards of Textiles and 45 pounds of Metal.

#### Breastplate
A shirt of hearty platemail that protects the vital organs, but little else. Worn over a suit of simple Leather armor (sold separately).

Created with 25 pounds of Metal.

#### Halfplate
A half-suit of hearty platemail that protects the vital organs, and much of the front of a fighter, worn over a thick leather undersuit.

Created with 6 sq. yards of Textiles and 40 pounds of Metal.
