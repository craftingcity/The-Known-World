The All-Father Annam is the firstborn of the Three Fates, and the first Titan of things. Born to the empty cosmos, Annam would take his form apart and sew his pieces together into something new; physical form. At first, his work was crude, forming the simple elemental planes of Fire, Water, Air, and Earth, but quickly, his work improved. He would go on to give his brother [[Asmodeus]] a gift; [[Baator]], a place all his own. Asmodeus took his gift kindly, and built bloody horrors upon it.

Quickly realizing his mistake, Annam would attempt to right this by building the planes [[Mechanis]] and [[Elysium]], of Law and Good, and placing them even beyond his own reach, such that [[Asmodeus]] and the grotesque amalgam known as [[Demogorgon]] would be unable to harm them.

As Annam continued, he would make enemies of the so-called [[Immortal Dragons]] he birthed in his elemental experiments. As conflict boiled between them, a challenge was issued between the Dragons and Annam, where they would battle in the center of all things. In this battle, each of the Dragons would falter and fail, excepting [[Tetrimoc]], the last of his kind to challenge their Father. The clashed, and in this moment, it was Annam who faltered. 

Tetrimoc ripped open Annam's chest, breaking his ribcage to it's marrow, rendering his flesh and organs disconnected from his form. In a death-throe, the [[All-Father Annam]] grasped [[Tetrimoc]] and shoved him into his now-destroyed torso, expelled all his effort and energy, and died. At the center of the multiverse, [[Alaturmen]] formed.

As [[Tetrimoc]] falls battered and bloodied into what was once his father's body, Time comes to [[Alaturmen]].
