### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they had escaped their conflict in [[The Holyl'nds]] and turned to [[Coalition City]] for a period of recovery, where they engaged in commerce and conversation with the citizens of the City. Their eyes were caught, for reasons each their own, by a series of board-postings that alluded to some ongoings in [[The Deserts of Tetrimoc]], at the legendary mountain we call [[Kailkeep]].

In addition to the legend you know - that the draconic god-king [[Tetrimoc]] was cut upon the tip of the mountain upon his descent into Alaturmen - the postage suggested that a large bounty of gold and magic awaits those who would come to claim it, but also that a number of people are concerned that their friends or family have gone missing around that area. Armed with this information and the equipment you procured in [[Coalition City]], you have travelled west-north-west along the [[Deakred Range]] and into the Desert. 

Today is the 6th day of Drying in the 106th year, and before you stands the large and lonely stone mass we call [[Kailkeep]]. Across the miles long foot of the mountain, it's dusty red stone mixes with the pale yellow sand of the ground you walk on, and for as wide as Kailkeep is, it is thrice as tall, imposing a long sharp shadow across the [[North Dunes]]. As you observe, the mountain seems to be a force of nature, untouched by the civilization it is surrounded by - excepting the relatively small square tunnel in front of you. It is dark, unlit by any artificial material, but not unwelcoming. 

What do you do?
