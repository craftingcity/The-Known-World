### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they were transporting a caravan of goods to the city of [[Wychway]] in an effort to relieve the pressures of wartime - on their journey however, minutes after they crossed into the Holyl'nd - they were attacked by a squadron of soldiers bearing the marks of the Church of the White Flame.

The combat was brutal. Corpses pumped full of crossbow bolts litter the road here, and much of the supplies once carried by the carts has been ruined against the bloody dirt. Where the forest was quiet before, it now feels full of silence. You and the rest of the survivors of your caravan are squeezed together in the singular surviving cart. Some of them have begun to quietly cry, while others are visibly dissociating. 

You four, however, have kept your wits about you. You can make a choice here. Do you retreat to the safety of the Kingdom of Prosper and willfully fail your contracted mission, or do you push forward to deliver what little remains of the caravan to the city that so desperately needs it?

### What does the World Know?
#### To Wychway!
The people of Wychway need any help they can get. They remain the frontline settlement of Arthurian forces in the Northern Conflicts, and they need people more than anything.

They'll get paid a modest sum of silver (like 40sp each) when they deliver the cart, even though little of the goods have survived, and then be dismissed into the city, for the most part. 

#### Retreat!
Though they may feel guilt, the survivors accept that they have failed, and will return home.
