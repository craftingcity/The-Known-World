### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they had traveled to the Temple of Flowers in [[Mountain Hall]] to meet with the renown fiendologist [[Fulton Shepherd]]. Once arrived, he asked that you remove a group of 'Loyalists' from a local hangout - which you promptly set ablaze. In return, he discussed with you your plans in regard to finding and destroying the Arbiter known to you as 'the Notadeer'. 

For as little as you know about the Hells of [[Baator]] and it's Devils, you have been told that they follow a strict bureaucracy, one that create and keep an incident report or a receipt of some kind - hopefully containing some actionable information regarding the Arbiter you seek. Fulton has suggested that, once you are as prepared as you can be, you might planeswalk from Alaturmen and into Baator, through the layers it contains, to either the record or the Arbiter, and then return, victorious. Another option floated was that in preparation for travel into [[Baator]], you would summon a Lesser Devil under relatively safe conditions and question it. 

You stand in the Temple of Flowers, in the city of [[Mountain Hall]], in the evening of the 6th day of Drying in the 106th year. What do you do?
