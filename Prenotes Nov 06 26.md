### Intro

When last we left our heroes, they were accosted in the city of [[Kamarios]] by Thieves of rival Nobility as the party gathered a package (of what we can only assume to be drugs) that they were ordered to collect and return to Cobalt's clutches. The party fought hard as the bar of this town's Silver Unicorn erupted into chaos, where blaster-fire and blades were popping and slashing from both sides of the conflict. Blood was spilled. Many Thieves would find this a dishonorable combat, but it is a combat won for the clutches of Cobalt. [[Kamarios]] remains loyally blue. Today that is honorable enough.

With your package secured, and the victims of this scuffle tended to, you are led outside by your squad-lead, Tarik, to begin your journey home. Upon your exiting, Eden directs each of us to view the skyline. To the north, high in the air, we see a company of Gleaming Wyvern Riders. Having been released from [[The Golden City]], they soar upon the backs of silver-scaled sky-snakes holding long lances and spears, flying westward towards the [[Lord's Folly]]. You have each witnessed the Riders before, in pairs or alone they patrol the area around the Golden City, causing significant trouble to your operations when they do, but never have you seen them in numbers such as these. 

Tarik speaks first; "Well, shit. Looks like the Claws are making a move. We should get back to Cobalt."
