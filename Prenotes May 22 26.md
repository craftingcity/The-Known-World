### Intro

When last we left our heroes, the party was infiltrating the Vault deep under [[Coalition City]] in search of any tools or weapons that would prepare them for their planned expedition into the [[Baator|Nine Hells of Baator]]. As the heist continued, the party gathered information that suggested a portable device that would transport you to or from the plane, they quickly moved to find and gather this "Worldbinding". After significant effort, they had found what they were looking for. And then, in an attempt to extract the device, a mistake was made, and the Worldbinding opened. As it did, as if they were waiting for you, the voice of the Notadeer commanded; "Come to me".
